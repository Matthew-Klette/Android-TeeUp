# TeeUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

TeeUp ("we", "the app") is a student project built for the Programming 3D (PROG7314) module at The
Independent Institute of Education. This policy explains what TeeUp collects, why, and the
choices you have. It is written to meet South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act
(POPIA) and Google Play's requirements.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address and sign-in identifier, handled by Firebase
  Authentication (Google). We never see your Google or email password.
- **Golf profile:** your display name, handicap index, home course, pace of play and notification
  preferences.
- **Activity in the app:** the groups you host or join, join requests, rounds and hole-by-hole
  scores, and the golfers you save as playing partners.
- **Device push token:** a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, so we can send you notifications.

## What stays on your phone

- **Location:** GPS is used only on your device to show distances on the hole map. It is never
  sent to our servers.
- **Profile photo:** stored only on your device. You can take it with the camera or pick it from your gallery; TeeUp asks for camera permission only when you choose to take a photo, and the picture is never uploaded.
- **Offline copies:** recently loaded data and rounds waiting to upload are kept in the app's
  private storage and are cleared when you sign out (queued rounds upload first).
- **Biometrics:** fingerprint or face data never leaves Android's secure hardware. TeeUp only
  receives a yes or no result.

## How we use it

To run the app: matching you with groups, showing your name, handicap and pace to golfers in the
same group, calculating round statistics, and sending the notifications you've switched on. We
don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for profiling.

## Who we share it with

Other TeeUp users see your display name, handicap and pace of play when you're in the same group
or search for partners. Our service providers process data on our behalf: Google Firebase
(authentication, push notifications, Realtime Database), Microsoft Azure (API hosting) and
Supabase (PostgreSQL database).

## Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Every API request must carry a
valid Firebase sign-in token, and database credentials are never stored in the app.

## Your choices and rights

- Change your profile and notification preferences at any time in Profile.
- Turn off notifications in Android settings, or deny the notification permission.
- Sign out at any time, which removes this device's push token.
- Ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing the support address on the Play Store
  listing. We will respond within 30 days.

## Children

TeeUp is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note it in the app's release notes.
