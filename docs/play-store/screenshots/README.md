# Play Store screenshots

Capture these on the physical phone used for the demo video (Power + Volume Down), at the phone's
native resolution (portrait, 9:16 or taller). Name them in listing order:

1. `01-home.png`: Home with open groups
2. `02-group-detail.png`: a group's detail with members and Request to Join
3. `03-hole-map.png`: the live GPS hole map during a round
4. `04-round-summary.png`: a round summary with net score and Stableford
5. `05-partners.png`: Playing Partners
6. `06-offline.png`: the "You're offline" banner with a round waiting to sync
7. `07-notifications.png`: Notifications after a push
8. `08-signin.png`: Sign-in with Google, email and biometrics

Then add them to the Play Console under *Main store listing → Phone screenshots*.
